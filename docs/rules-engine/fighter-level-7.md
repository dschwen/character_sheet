# Fighter level 7 and Fighting Style choices

The Champion's Additional Fighting Style is a source-linked, level-7 feat choice. The initial
Fighter style is also a choice rather than a permanently forced Defense feat. All four styles
in the pinned SRD are loaded: Archery, Defense, Great Weapon Fighting, and Two-Weapon Fighting.
Choices in the shared `fighting-style` group must be distinct. The evaluator rejects missing,
early, duplicate, wrong-owner, and unrecorded selections and traces their granting features.

The initial style can be replaced on a Fighter level-up. Advancement removes a replaced
feature-granted feat while preserving unrelated feats, possessions, and play state. The
additional style is not replaceable under the current source-derived metadata. Loading old
single-class Fighters records their existing style in the missing historical choice, without
inventing a new feat or changing current state; explicit records are never overwritten.

Defense retains its armor-only +1 AC rule. Archery applies a source-linked +2 attack bonus only
to ranged weapons, not melee weapons (including those with Thrown). Combat damage controls now
offer source-linked two-handed and extra Light-weapon modes. Versatile weapons use their larger
listed die only when two-handed use is declared; two-handed-property weapons default to that mode.
An equipped shield blocks the two-handed mode. The normal/manual mode retains ordinary listed
damage without applying these conditional styles.

Great Weapon Fighting treats weapon-damage dice below 3 as 3 only in an eligible two-handed
melee mode. The fullscreen and fallback rollers share the same resolver, retaining both raw and
effective dice and explaining each adjustment. Critical hits double dice, not modifiers, and
the treatment applies to the additional critical dice. Ranged two-handed weapons do not qualify.

Extra Light-weapon attacks omit positive ability modifiers unless Two-Weapon Fighting applies;
negative modifiers and separate Rage damage remain. The mode checks for a different owned Light
weapon, including a second copy represented by quantity. Attack-action prerequisites, once-per-turn
limits, Bonus Action/Nick timing, hand usage, mounted exceptions, and additional attack damage
riders remain manually adjudicated. Selecting a mode declares its context; it does not spend an
action or apply damage to a target. The structured weapon dice are automated, not encounter turns.

Fighter creation and same-class advancement now reach level 7. Numeric progression, subclass
feature grants, Second Wind, Action Surge, mastery capacity, and existing ASI choices continue
to come from the package. Owning the selected mastery weapons is still a warning, not a
requirement to discard a legal mastery selection. Browser interaction verification is outstanding.
