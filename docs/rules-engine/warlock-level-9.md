# Warlock level 9

Creation and same-class advancement now reach level 9. The pinned SRD table (page 71)
grants Proficiency Bonus +4, seven invocations, three cantrips, ten ordinary prepared
spells, and two fifth-level Pact slots. The generated builder includes level-9 skill
and invocation counts; no Warlock-specific frontend calculation was added.

Advancement moves spent fourth-level Pact slots into the fifth-level pool, preserving
zero, one, or two spent uses. It is not a rest. Earlier acquisition history, feats,
equipment, currency, current HP, and expended resources are retained. Both Short and
Long Rest restore Pact slots. Magical Cunning restores one of the two fifth-level
slots, independently of other resources. Lower-level spells still spend fifth-level
Pact slots, not obsolete lower-level slots.

Fiend Spells (page 76) adds Geas and Insect Plague automatically. Contact Patron
(page 72) always prepares Contact Other Plane and grants one free casting per Long
Rest. Use its **Free cast · Contact Patron** button to spend that use without a Pact
slot. Confirmation instructs you to contact your patron and automatically succeed on
the spell's saving throw, without rolling. This benefit is not attached to ordinary
slot or ritual casts; patron answers remain GM-adjudicated. Short Rest does not restore
the Contact Patron use. Its resource and spending trace retain the source feature.

Visions of Distant Realms (page 74) is a default seventh invocation. Its **Cast at will**
button casts Arcane Eye without a spell slot or finite resource and starts Concentration.
Repeated casts remain available. Perception and eye movement are manually adjudicated.
Removing the invocation removes that automatic spell access. With this invocation,
the default Fiend has twelve automatic spells (ten Fiend, Contact Other Plane, Arcane
Eye), all outside its ten ordinary preparation selections.

The shared free-casting schema supports either an explicit spell list or the existing
selected-option spell grants, and either a finite resource or at-will casting. Ambiguous
definitions are rejected. Authored guidance is returned only for the applicable free
cast and shown in confirmation. Invalid selections cannot authorize a feature-specific
free casting. Existing Circle of the Land recovery casting remains resource-backed.

One earlier invocation may be replaced alongside the seventh new selection. Level
and invocation prerequisites remain enforced, including Lifedrinker's Pact of the Blade
and Gift of the Protectors' Pact of the Tome prerequisites. These and other invocations
still have assisted or display-only effects; this slice does not automate pact weapon
binding, Hit Die healing, Book of Shadows names, repeatable invocation subchoices,
all fifth-level spell effects, or component costs. Preparation remains level-up-only.
Levels 10+, independent semantic review, and rendered browser verification remain open.
