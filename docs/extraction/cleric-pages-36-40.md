# Cleric extraction, pages 36–40

The Cleric slice is generated from the pinned SRD 5.2.1 pages 36–40 and the normalized spell
description corpus from pages 107–175.

The parser validates all 20 progression rows, proficiency scaling, Channel Divinity uses, cantrip
and preparation counts, nine spell-slot columns, eleven class features, five Life Domain features,
the 109-entry Cleric spell list, and the Life Domain always-prepared spell table. Divine Order is
represented by explicit Protector and Thaumaturge option entities.

Seventeen representative Cleric spells receive source-linked casting, component, duration,
Concentration, Ritual, attack, save, damage, and healing fields. Together with shared spell
entities, they cover the reviewed level-5 fixture's cantrips, nine selected prepared spells, and six
always-prepared Life Domain spells.
