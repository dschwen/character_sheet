Paladin, Ranger, Rogue, Sorcerer, Wizard, Human, Soldier, Origin Feat, Fighting Style, spell, weapon,
and armor proof-of-concept content.

The package contains the Barbarian, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Rogue, Sorcerer,
and Wizard class progressions; their extracted class features and scaling tables; Champion; Life
Domain; Circle of the Land; Warrior of the Open Hand; Oath of Devotion; Hunter; Thief; Draconic
Sorcery; Evoker; Human; Soldier; relevant feats; the equipment tables needed by the reviewed
fixtures; complete Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger, Sorcerer, and Wizard spell lists; and 66
source-linked structured spell descriptions. Rage, Unarmored Defense, Fighter level 1–5, Champion
level-3 features, Channel Divinity, Preserve Life, Wild Shape, Wild Companion, Land’s Aid, Monk
level 1–5, Paladin level 1–5, Ranger level 1–5, Rogue level 1–5, Sorcerer level 1–5, Defense, and
Savage Attacker have schema-valid proposed rules with integration tests. Cleric, Druid, Paladin,
Ranger, Sorcerer, and Wizard spellcasting is evaluated from structured class, subclass, and spell
fields. All entities remain `STRUCTURED` until their semantics and tests are reviewed and approved.
